Privacy Policy for Kattjakten

Kattjakten, developed by Pelican Games AB, values the privacy of our players, especially children. This game is designed to be a safe experience and does not collect, store, or share any personal data (such as names, emails, contacts, or location).

To help us balance difficulty and improve level design, we collect limited, non-personal gameplay metrics using GameAnalytics. This includes:

  • A randomly generated, app-specific ID (not tied to your identity, real-world data, or device IDFA).
  • Anonymous gameplay metrics, specifically how long it takes players to complete quests and find hidden items.

We do not display third-party advertisements, nor do we track users across other apps or websites. All telemetry data is used solely for internal game optimization.

If our practices ever change, we will update this privacy policy and inform users accordingly. If you have questions, please contact Pelican Games AB at info@pelicangames.se.
